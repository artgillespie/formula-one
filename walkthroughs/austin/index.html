        Sources & credits

        • Telemetry, lap, sector & result data: OpenF1 (fetched live in your browser, with a saved snapshot as fallback). Unofficial; not associated with Formula 1.
        • Track outline & corner positions: F1 live-timing circuit data via MultiViewer's public API.
        • Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen/Tilezen), from the USGS 3D Elevation Program (public domain).
        • Ground features, run-off paint, buildings & grandstands: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
        • 3D rendering: three.js (MIT), including its Preetham sky model. Sun position is computed for race day, 25 October 2026.
        • Photographs: see individual credits.

        Corner names follow common usage. Track length shown is the official 5.513 km. Corner speeds and gears are measured from the pole laps. Sector lines are placed using the official sector split times. Heights are true scale, with no vertical exaggeration.
